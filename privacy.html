Last updated: September 2026

This page covers the information you share with Elevate Coaching LLC when you apply for Built Different.

What we collect

The application form asks for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Instagram handle, if you choose to share it
  • Your height and weight
  • What you've tried before for training and nutrition
  • Your goals and why you're applying now

How it's handled

Form submissions are received and stored through Netlify Forms, the service that hosts this site.

The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

The video at the top of the page is hosted on YouTube, and Google may set cookies when you play it.

How it's used

Only to review your application and follow up with you about it. Your information is never sold.

Deleting your information

Email adam@elevatecoaching90.com and ask for your data to be deleted. That's all it takes.
